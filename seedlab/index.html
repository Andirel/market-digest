    No black box

    Sources, assumptions & limits

    Allocation is a rule-based model

    The model filters by horizon, risk ceiling, exclusions and optional cash-flow discipline. It retains up to three eligible assets per theme, weights themes by your stated conviction, then applies theme, crypto and single-seed caps. Unassignable capital goes to the Treasury anchor. Scores are editorial heuristics, not expected-return estimates.

    Core capital is split between VTI and SGOV. Below five years, this model puts new capital in SGOV rather than stretching the growth thesis. A conservative preset is relative to the growth universe; even broad equity funds can fall sharply.

    Overlap matters

    AI, power, space and crypto can all suffer in the same risk-off market. HOOD and COIN add crypto-linked business exposure even though they are stocks. VTI also owns some of these companies; the model does not perform fund look-through optimization.

    Why these 30?

    A curated starting universe of 24 equities and 6 tokens spanning AI infrastructure, power, space and defense, finance, healthcare and crypto. These are research candidates, not a comprehensive market screen. VTI and SGOV are additional anchors, not high-upside seeds. This is a fixed research universe, not a claim that these are the market's best 30 assets.

    One consistent market-data basis

    Last completed daily closes, not live executable quotes. Yahoo Finance data is retrieved through yfinance. Sources and timestamps are included for every asset. The provider can rate-limit or omit fields; missing values stay unavailable and failed updates retain their original dates. Valuation and estimate fields are provider figures at retrieval, not independently audited fundamentals.

    MACD and RSI are not valuations

    MACD (12,26,9) uses EMA12 − EMA26 and its EMA9 signal. Histogram sign indicates relative momentum; a positive line alone is not a buy signal. Wilder RSI(14) below 30 is labeled oversold; above 70 is overbought. Either can persist. All calculations use daily bars, with up to two years of warmup.

    Analysts and weekly refreshes

    Equity consensus uses the latest available Strong buy → Strong sell distribution: 1 = Strong buy, 5 = Strong sell. Display labels round the weighted score to the nearest category. Twelve-month targets are analyst opinions, not forecasts from Seedlab. Crypto has no comparable sell-side consensus in this feed.

    Weekly Monday morning refreshes update prices, indicators and available analyst data. Editorial theses remain dated research notes and are not automatically re-underwritten.
